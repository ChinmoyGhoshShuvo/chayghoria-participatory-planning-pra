**Title:** Participatory Rural Appraisal, Chayghoria Village (Khulna)

**Summary:** I led a 5-member PRA team working with residents of a flood-prone village on the Rupsa River. Together we built social, resource and mobility maps, a transect walk and a crop calendar, and ranked local problems. Residents ranked lack of flood management as the most pressing issue, so we designed a 5.3 km community-built drainage network and estimated its cost.
